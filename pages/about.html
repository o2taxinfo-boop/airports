Who We Are

Your Trusted Airport Taxi in Thame

O2Taxi is a family-run private hire taxi service based in Thame, Oxfordshire. We are the go-to airport transfer service for residents across Thame and South Oxfordshire, trusted by local families, businesses and visitors alike.

We specialise in airport transfers to Heathrow, Gatwick, Luton, Stansted and Birmingham, but we also cover local journeys, train station transfers, long-distance travel and parcel deliveries. Whatever your travel need, O2Taxi is here to help.

We are fully licensed by South Oxfordshire District Council. All our vehicles are regularly maintained, clean and comfortable. Our drivers are experienced, fully licensed, friendly and committed to excellent customer service — from the moment you book to the moment you arrive.

📍 Our Service Area

We are based in Thame and serve all of South Oxfordshire including Haddenham, Chinnor, Long Crendon, Watlington, Great Milton, Chalgrove, Tetsworth, Stadhampton, Lewknor, Ewelme and many surrounding villages. We also cover Buckinghamshire border communities including Princes Risborough, Bledlow, Ickford and Worminghall.

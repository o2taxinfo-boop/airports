Privacy Policy

How O2Taxi collects and uses your information

Last updated: January 2025

1. Who we are

O2Taxi is a private hire taxi service based in Thame, Oxfordshire, licensed by South Oxfordshire District Council. Our website is airport.o2taxi.com. You can contact us at 07923 360048.

2. What information we collect

When you make a booking or enquiry, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Pickup and drop-off address
  • Travel date and time
  • Flight details (where provided)
  • Number of passengers

3. How we use your information

We use your information solely to confirm and fulfil your booking, contact you about your journey, and provide customer service. We do not use your information for marketing without your consent, and we do not sell your data to third parties.

4. How we store your information

Booking details are stored securely and retained only as long as necessary to fulfil your journey and meet any legal obligations. WhatsApp and phone communications are held on our devices and are not shared externally.

5. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct or request deletion of your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at 07923 360048 or via WhatsApp.

6. Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies or analytics tools. No cookies are set on your device.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us: 07923 360048 | WhatsApp
